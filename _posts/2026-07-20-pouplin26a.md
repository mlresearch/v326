abstract: This second edition of GRaM, the workshop on Geometry-grounded Representation
  Learning and Generative Modeling, was held at ICLR 2026 in Rio de Janeiro. GRaM
  is built on the principle of grounding in geometry — that machine learning models
  should respect the geometric structure of their data, from symmetries and manifolds
  to graphs and non-Euclidean spaces, rather than treat their inputs as plain vectors.
  This year we asked whether such structure holds up under the theme scale and simplicity.
